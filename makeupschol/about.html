Started in a bedroom. Booked out most Saturdays now.

Marlowe & Muse began in 2021 as a one-woman kit and a spare room in Melville. Five years on, the philosophy hasn't changed.

Our history

Marlow started doing makeup for friends' weddings while working a full-time retail job. What began as a favour for a bridesmaid turned into referrals, then a waiting list, then a full diary. In 2023 she left retail to do makeup artistry full-time.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To make every client feel like the most natural, most confident version of themselves.

Vision: To be the first name Johannesburg brides and creatives think of when they want makeup that respects real skin.

Who we work with

Brides & bridal parties

Engaged clients planning weddings across Gauteng.

Photographers & creatives

Photographers and small agencies booking makeup for shoots.

Individuals & families

Matric dance, birthdays and festive-season clients.

Meet the artist

Marlow Adeyemi

Marlow trained at a private makeup academy in Johannesburg and has since worked backstage at SA Fashion Week, on independent short films, and on more weddings than she's kept count of. She's known for a light hand and a calm presence on a busy morning.
